Why WordPress

Because it is everywhere. A script written for WordPress works against millions of sites, so the effort of writing it is paid back a million times. The attacker is not choosing me, he is choosing the biggest crowd he can find, and my site happens to be in the crowd.

The irony is that this blog has no WordPress at all. The requests arrive for files that never existed, for plugins that were never installed, for an administration panel that was never there. Every single attempt is answered with the same static page, which is a polite way of saying that the door they are pushing is a wall.

What the police can and cannot do

A threat to call the police is a joke between the lines, and everyone involved knows it. An attack from a rotating address, on a site with no damage and no victims, will not produce an investigation anywhere in the world. The law is slow and the packet is gone.

What is left is the boring answer: close what is open, ban what repeats, and keep the surface small. Frustration is a feeling, and a firewall is a decision. I prefer the decision.
