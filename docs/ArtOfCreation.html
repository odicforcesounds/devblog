The equation of application

01Why 1 + 1 = 1 OR 3 is scientifically true

Defended as scientific event: In systems science this is called emergence — hydrogen (1) + oxygen (1) = water (1 new substance with properties neither gas has). In psychology: explicit reasoning + associative imagination = insight, a single decision that contains both and transcends both. In Taoism: Tao begets One, One begets Two, Two begets Three, Three begets the ten thousand things (Tao Te Ching, ch.42).

SCIENCE • CHEMISTRY / SYSTEMS

1 + 1 = 1 — Fusion

Two neurons fire together and wire together (Hebb). Two ideas fuse into one memory engram. Marriage of gametes: two cells become one zygote. Fusion is real: quantity collapses into new unity.

SCIENCE • EMERGENCE

1 + 1 = 3 — The Third Appears

Parents (2) create child (3rd). Thesis + antithesis = synthesis. Sound wave + eardrum = music-experience — the experience is a third entity, neither air nor flesh alone. Application is always that third.

Creative interpretation for the reader to verify: Clap once. Left hand is imagination, right hand is logic. Neither hand alone makes the sound. The clap is the “3”. You just proved Tao with your own body. Every invention — song, bridge, apology — is a clap.
The essence of Taoism

02Living Knowledge: Will + Causality of Nature

Taoism says wu-wei — effortless action, aligning will with nature’s causality. Mechanics of living knowledge: knowledge is not stored, it is grown. Like music, it lives only while resonating.

BIOLOGY

Knowledge is metabolic

Memories are re-built proteins at synapses every recall. Forgetting is not failure; it is pruning, like a garden. Taoist “emptying the mind” maps exactly onto synaptic homeostasis — the brain must forget to stay efficient.

PHYSICS • RESONANCE

Resonance, frequency, flow

Everything that persists oscillates: heart ~1 Hz, breath ~0.2 Hz, alpha brain 8–12 Hz, atoms at terahertz. When two oscillators meet, they entrain (Huygens’ clocks, fireflies, menstrual synchrony debates, choir breathing). Inspiration literally means “breathing-in-together”. Music is entrainment made audible.

Therefore: will (directed attention) + causality (laws of oscillation) = sustained harmony. The sage does not force the string; he tunes it.

If time is built within ourselves

03Time is Our Selves, as Result of Time Itself

Defended as scientific event: Physics has no universal “now” (relativity). Neuroscience shows time is constructed: the brain binds smell, sound, vision arriving at different speeds into one “present” ~80–150 ms wide. Dopamine and attention stretch or compress it — fear slows time, routine erases it. Amnesiacs live in an eternal now. Without memory, there is no time.

Creative interpretation: You are a drum. The universe strikes; you resonate; that resonance is your time. Death of resonance is not death of universe, only of your measure. So “Time is built within” = your hippocampus + cortex + heartbeat compose tempo. Change breath, change music, change time. Meditators and musicians prove it daily.

Brain • Psychology • Sound • Memories

04The Instrument: Brain that Sings to Itself

NEUROSCIENCE

Brain

86 billion neurons, no central conductor — harmony emerges from rhythm. Gamma synchrony binds features into one conscious scene. Trauma desynchronizes; sleep re-synchronizes. Healing = re-tuning.

ACOUSTICS • AFFECT

Sound

Sound bypasses logic and enters the amygdala in ~12 ms. A mother’s voice lowers cortisol; dissonance raises skin conductance. Cymatics shows vibration organizes matter into mandalas. If vibration organizes sand, what does lifelong inner speech organize in you?

PSYCHOLOGY

Memories

Every recall re-consolidates — memory is re-imagined, not replayed. False memories implant as easily as true ones. Buddhism knew: clinging to a re-written past is suffering. Science agrees: update the story, update the self.

PSYCHOLOGY

Psychology of attention

What you rehearse, you become. Negativity bias was survival; gratitude practice measurably rewires it. The ancients called it karma / sowing-reaping; labs call it neuroplasticity + reinforcement learning.

Will • Desires • Creativity • Creation and Destruction

05Fire that Cooks and Burns

Defended: Desire is dopamine prediction, not pleasure itself — the brain wants more than it likes. Will (prefrontal control) steers that fire. Creativity is controlled destruction: to make a sculpture you destroy stone; to make an idea you destroy an assumption. Shiva / Kali, Christ’s “die to live”, Laozi’s “to attain fullness, first be emptied” all describe synaptic pruning + creative destruction (Schumpeter in economics, apoptosis in biology).

Reader experiment: Hold a desire (sweet, phone, anger) for 90 seconds without acting, breathing slowly. Watch it peak and dissolve like a wave. You just observed: desire ≠ command. Will is not suppression — it is surfing. Creation needs destruction of the wave’s dominance.
Buddhism • Journey to the West

06The Monkey and the Buddha Are One Head

In Journey to the West, Sun Wukong (Monkey — restless, brilliant, desirous mind) accompanies Tang Sanzang / the Buddha-nature (calm, compassionate direction) to fetch sutras. They fight constantly. Neither reaches the West alone.

BUDDHIST PSYCHOLOGY

Monkey = Default Mode + Dopamine

Mind-wandering, craving, cleverness, comparison. Necessary: without Monkey, no exploration, no humor, no escape from tigers. Untrained, he burns the temple down.

BUDDHIST PSYCHOLOGY

Buddha = Executive + Compassion network

Still attention, witnessing, vow to liberate all beings. Necessary: without Buddha, brilliance has no compass. Unbalanced, he becomes cold detachment.

The connection: The golden headband (Tightening spell) is mindfulness itself — not punishment but feedback. Neuroscience: meditation strengthens prefrontal–limbic coupling; Monkey is not killed, he is employed as protector. Enlightenment = Monkey’s power + Buddha’s direction fused: 1 + 1 = 1 pilgrim, producing the 3rd: the liberated journey.

How society works • How individuals behave

08Society is a Choir, Not a Crowd

Individual = oscillator (habits, traumas, songs). Society = coupled oscillators. Three laws explain almost everything:

BEHAVIORAL SCIENCE

1. Imitation entrains

Mirror neurons, social proof, algorithmic feeds. You hum what your village hums. Change the loudest frequency (media, leaders, mothers) and the choir shifts key — for good or ill.

GAME THEORY

2. Incentives tune

People defect when cheaters prosper (Prisoner’s Dilemma). Societies heal when cooperation pays (tit-for-tat, restorative justice, cooperative ownership). Christ’s forgiveness + Tao’s non-punitive flow are optimal strategies, proven in Axelrod tournaments.

EDUCATION • EPISTEMICS

3. Stories synchronize memory

Nations are shared memories. If history is revenge-myth, brains rehearse threat. If history is “how we learned”, brains rehearse repair. Whoever controls the songbook controls the nervous system of millions. Hence the need for open books, wikis, blogs, art — many tuners, no monopoly.
