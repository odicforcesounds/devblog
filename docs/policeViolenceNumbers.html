Two Sides of Violence: People and Police

This document reviews the numbers published by official and independent sources on violent encounters involving police officers and the people they detain, arrest, or patrol.

It does not defend either side. It lists two sets of victims: the civilians whose deaths or injuries are attributed to police action, and the officers who are assaulted or killed in the line of duty. It does not combine them into a competition. It keeps them side by side so the reader can see that every number is a person.

All figures refer to the last ten years (2016–2026) and are drawn from agencies that publish raw counts, not estimates that hide uncertainty.
