One Human Dance

One body, one rhythm, a thousand schools. The shelves below gather dance and the martial arts together, because both are the same training: balance, step, breath and the discipline of a teacher. Every entry names the people who developed the form and the century it is first recorded in.

What an era means here. The date beside a style is the first record of the form, not the day it began — a dance can be centuries older than the sentence that mentions it, and a martial art usually names the century its school was founded, not the fighting it grew out of.

Opening the catalogue…

Names, first sentences and links come from Wikipedia; the shelves, the culture and the era are this catalogue's own grouping. A style that is missing means the shelf has not reached it yet, not that it did not happen.
