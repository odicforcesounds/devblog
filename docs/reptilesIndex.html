Reptiles and Snakes

The reptile shelf of the animal catalogue, pulled out on its own: the four snakes the catalogue holds, the lizards that share their order, the turtles, the crocodilians and the one tuatara. Everything here comes from the same JSON the big index reads, so the scaled half and the whole can never disagree.

Snakes are not a separate order. They share Squamata with the lizards, which is why they are listed below as their own shelf and again, family by family, inside it.
