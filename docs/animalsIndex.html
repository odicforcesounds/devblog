The Animal Index

From a blue whale the length of a bus to a tardigrade smaller than a grain of salt, the kingdom is one long argument about how to stay alive — with wings, with teeth, with armour, with a colony, with a shell. This page sorts them by class, order and family, and gives each one a photograph, its numbers and, where a recording exists, its voice.

One absence, on purpose. Humans are not in this catalogue. Everything else in Animalia is fair game, from the great apes down to the invertebrates that outnumber all of us.
