The Animal Index

From a blue whale the length of a bus to a tardigrade smaller than a grain of salt, the kingdom is one long argument about how to stay alive — with wings, with teeth, with armour, with a colony, with a shell. This page sorts them by class, order and family, and gives each one a photograph, its numbers and, where a recording exists, its voice.

One absence, on purpose. Humans are not in this catalogue. Everything else in Animalia is fair game, from the great apes down to the invertebrates that outnumber all of us.

Two companion indexes take the catalogue breed by breed: the dog index, from the wolf to every breed, and the cat index, from the wildcat to every breed.

Type two letters or more; the hundred order files are read only when you search.

Photographs, introductions, measurements and recordings come from Wikipedia, its media library and Wikidata, fetched through the public APIs when this catalogue was compiled; each card links back to the article and the file it uses. A missing figure is missing at the source, not guessed here.
