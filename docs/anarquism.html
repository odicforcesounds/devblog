Level 0 — Orientation • No prerequisites

L0 Is This Manual For You?

Short answer: if you want useful work, peace, and freedom together — yes. Anarchism is the political tradition that says: nobody should rule anyone else, and we can still run complex societies — through voluntary cooperation. The word comes from Greek an-arkhia = “without a ruler”, not “without order”.

WHAT ANARCHISM IS
  • A design method: replace command hierarchies with horizontal networks.
  • An economy: commons + cooperatives + mutual aid instead of boss-owned firms or state-owned monopolies.
  • A peace strategy: no armies of conquest, no police as rulers — community safety, mediation, restorative justice.
WHAT IT IS NOT
  • Not “everyone does whatever they want”. Freedom stops where domination starts.
  • Not chaos: Spanish collectives ran trains, hospitals and farms in 1936–39 with elected, recallable delegates.
  • Not pacifism-at-all-costs for all currents: most anarchists defend self-defence but oppose war, conscription and invasion.
PROMOTIONAL NOTE — WHY TRY IT? Because hierarchy wastes talent. In a cooperative, the person who does the job designs the job. Studies of worker cooperatives (Mondragón in Spain, Emilia-Romagna in Italy) show higher survival rates and pay equity. You get usefulness + dignity + peace — not one at the expense of the others.

Level 1 — Theory • Unlock by passing L0

L1 No Rulers, Not No Rules: The 6 Core Concepts

Think of anarchism as an operating system with six kernel modules. Learn them once, you can read every later chapter.

1. Mutual aid (Kropotkin)

Peter Kropotkin, observing Siberian animals and peasant communes, argued cooperation — not cut-throat competition — drives evolution (Mutual Aid: A Factor of Evolution, 1902). Technical form: pooled funds, tool libraries, community kitchens, strike support. Everyone contributes what they can, draws what they need in crisis.

2. Free association & voluntary federation

Individuals → affinity groups → neighbourhood assemblies → federations of assemblies. Higher levels cannot command lower ones; they coordinate. Delegates carry a written mandate and can be recalled instantly. This is the opposite of a party that disciplines its base.

3. Direct action

Solve the problem yourself instead of petitioning rulers: occupy empty housing to house people, create a free clinic, run a strike without union bosses. Direct action is not necessarily violent — most of it is constructive (build the alternative) and obstructive (block the harm).

4. Prefiguration

Build the future in the present. If you want a non-sexist, non-racist society, your assembly must be non-sexist and non-racist now: rotation of speaking, childcare at meetings, translation, veto against oppression.

5. Commons, not property-as-power

Distinction: personal possessions (your home, toothbrush) are respected; means of production (land, factories, platforms) should not give one person power over another. Solutions: cooperatives, community land trusts, open-source licenses.

6. Anti-domination (intersectional)

Class, state, patriarchy, racism, colonialism, ecocide are linked hierarchies. A factory takeover that keeps sexist shift rules has failed technically — it reproduced domination.

Level 2 — Economics • How to produce without bosses

L2 Production: Cooperatives, Commons & Planning from Below

Question every society must answer: who decides what is made, how, and who gets it? Capitalism answers: owners of capital. State socialism answers: party ministries. Anarchism answers: the producers and users together, federated.

A. The cooperative firm (technical spec)

  • Ownership: one worker = one vote. No outside shareholders.
  • Pay: ratio voted (e.g., Mondragón caps top-to-bottom around 6:1 vs 300:1 in US corporations).
  • Surplus: reserve + reinvestment + community fund, voted yearly.
  • Real examples: Mondragón (Spain, 70,000+ workers), Emilia-Romagna cooperatives (Italy, ~1/3 of regional GDP), CECOSESOLA (Venezuela, 20,000+ members, food + health).

B. The commons (Ostrom’s 8 rules, simplified)

Elinor Ostrom won the Nobel showing commons need not collapse. Rules: clear boundaries, proportional costs/benefits, collective monitoring, graduated sanctions, accessible conflict resolution. Applied to: water, forests, code, housing.

Level 3 — Governance • Decide fast without rulers

L3 Decision-Making: Consensus, Consent & Recall

Anarchists use three gears: (1) autonomy for personal/small matters, (2) majority or consent for routine coordination, (3) consensus for high-stakes shared commitments. No president needed — facilitation rotates.

Roles in an assembly (rotate every meeting)

  • Facilitator: keeps stack, not a chief.
  • Stack-taker: progressive stack — prioritises those rarely heard.
  • Vibes / mediator: watches fatigue, proposes breaks.
  • Note-taker: public minutes; decisions + who does what by when.

Level 5 — Field evidence • Real countries, real places

L5 Real Examples: Where It Worked, Where It Was Crushed

🇪🇸 SPAIN 1936–39 — THE LARGEST TESTAfter Franco’s coup, CNT-FAI workers collectivised 70%+ of Catalonian industry, Barcelona transport, hospitals; Aragon peasant communes federated. Production rose in many sectors; women’s organisation Mujeres Libres (30,000 members) ran schools/clinics. Crushed by Franco + Stalinist repression + war. Lesson: federations can run cities, but need anti-fascist defence accountable to assemblies.
🇺🇦 UKRAINE 1918–21 — MAKHNOVSHCHINANestor Makhno’s peasant-worker army + free soviets: land to peasants, factory committees, multilingual schools, voluntary mobilisation. Crushed by Bolsheviks after joint fight vs Whites. Lesson: alliance with authoritarians without guarantees = death. Betrayal by Reds = equal to betrayal by Whites.
🇲🇽 CHIAPAS (1994–) — ZAPATISTA CARACOLESIndigenous assemblies, Good Government Juntas, autonomous health/education (thousands of promotores), women’s Revolutionary Law. No caudillo rule; cargos rotate. Under siege but enduring 30+ years. Lesson: autonomy + dignity scale slowly and last.
SYRIA / ROJAVA (2012–) — DEMOCRATIC CONFEDERALISMAbdullah Öcalan + Kurdish movement adapted anarchist/federalist ideas (Bookchin): communes → councils → TEV-DEM, co-presidency (man+woman), women’s defence (YPJ), cooperatives, ecology academies. War + embargo; imperfections real, direction horizontal. Lesson: feminism + ecology + federation can govern millions under fire.
🇫🇷 FRANCE — LONG TRACEParis Commune 1871 (recallable delegates, separation church/state), May ’68 factory/university occupations, ZAD Notre-Dame-des-Landes (autonomous zone 2008–18 defeated airport), 1000+ SCOP cooperatives today. Shows anarchist methods inside a republic.
🇺🇸 USA — MUTUAL AID UNDER EMPIREFrom 1886 Haymarket martyrs to 1999 Seattle, Occupy 2011, 2020 mutual-aid networks (food, bail funds, street medics). US also shows the negative: COINTELPRO, McCarthyism, Iraq invasion — state repression by a democracy = equal to repression by any state.

Level 6 — Build • New ideas for peaceful, useful societies

L6 Build Yours: 7-Day Starter + 5 New Ideas

Your 7-day starter (copy-paste to your street)

  1. Day 1 — Map: list 10 neighbours, 5 tools to share, 3 empty spaces. Knock, introduce, note needs.
  2. Day 2 — Assembly #1: 60 min, rotating facilitator, one decision only (e.g., tool library). Publish minutes on paper + local forum.
  3. Day 3 — Commons: open a box/shelf of shared tools, seeds, books. Public ledger.
  4. Day 4 — Mutual aid: cook 20 extra portions; deliver to elders/sick. Record costs openly.
  5. Day 5 — Cooperative sketch: what could you sell together? Cleaning, repairs, care, code? Draft one-page statutes (1 worker = 1 vote).
  6. Day 6 — Safety: mediation pair + de-escalation numbers; agree: no calling armed force on neighbours except to stop immediate violence.
  7. Day 7 — Federate: invite adjacent street; exchange delegates with written mandates.

5 new ideas to try (promotional — steal them)

1. CARE COMMONSNeighbourhood fund pays caregivers in credits convertible to food/repairs. Care hours counted like factory hours. Pilots in Chiapas/Rojava show it cuts gender burden.
2. OPEN-BOOK LOGISTICSEvery cooperative publishes capacity, costs, stocks weekly (paper + local mesh). Overproduction and hoarding become visible — planning without spies.
3. RECALLABLE EVERYTHINGNot only politicians: building managers, moderators, treasurers — all mandated, rotated, recallable. Trust rises because power is revocable.
4. RESTORATIVE PATROLSUnarmed, mixed-gender, trained mediation teams replace punitive patrols. Night walks + lit spaces + safe houses. Measure success by harm repaired, not arrests.
5. MESH MEDIA (BE YOUR INDYMEDIA)Like Indybay taught: don’t beg corporate press — run open-publishing. A neighbourhood bulletin + audio + mesh chat, moderated by rotating assembly, federated with other zones. Truth from below.

Annex A — Where to continue (real platforms)

WWW Known Platforms & Media (go read them)

Anarchism lives in independent media, not party press. Start here — all open, most volunteer-run, federated or open-publishing:

  • Indybay / Indymedia network — indybay.org — San Francisco Bay Area Independent Media Center; historic node of the global Indymedia open-publishing network born in Seattle 1999. Post your own news, no corporate gatekeeper. Sister nodes worldwide (e.g., links via indymedia.org archives).
  • It’s Going Down (IGD) — itsgoingdown.org — North-American anarchist digital community center: news, podcasts, prisoner support. Often cross-posts Indybay reports.
  • CrimethInc. — crimethinc.com — Decentralised anarchist collective: books (Work, From Democracy to Freedom), posters, how-to guides. Beautiful, plain-language theory.
  • The Anarchist Library — theanarchistlibrary.org — Thousands of free texts: Kropotkin, Goldman, Bookchin, Öcalan, contemporary zines. Download, print, share.
  • libcom.org — libcom.org — Libertarian-communist library + history (Spain, Kronstadt, May ’68) + organising guides.
  • Freedom News (UK) — freedomnews.org.uk — Anarchist newspaper since 1886; daily web edition,place-based reporting.
  • CoopCycle / Platform Cooperativism — coopcycle.org — Federation of courier cooperatives owning their delivery app. Proof L2 works on smartphones.
  • Anarchist Federation aggregators & podcasts: search your region + “anarchist bookfair / infoshop” — physical infoshops remain the most resilient servers ever built (paper + soup).
HOW TO USE THEMRead three sources per event (one corporate, one state, one from below e.g. Indybay/IGD). Ask: who decides? who profits? who is recallable? That triple-check is the anarchist media literacy protocol.
