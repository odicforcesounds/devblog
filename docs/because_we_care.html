I don’t care who You are!!!

This means, I care about Your Privacy! ;)

Privacy is not a promise, it is an architecture

A promise can be broken, and a policy can be rewritten on a Tuesday afternoon. An architecture cannot. The reason I know nothing about you is not that I am a good person, it is that the site has no place to store anything: no accounts, no comments, no forms, no database, no cookies, no analytics script that phones home.

There is one thing a server always learns, because the network requires it: the address the request came from. I do not build a profile from it, I do not sell it, and I use it for exactly one purpose, which is to keep automated floods away from the text. That is the whole inventory.

Why Tor, VPN and proxies are welcome

If you need Tor to read a page about philosophy, the problem is not you, it is the network you are in. Blocking anonymity tools would protect an idea of a reader instead of the reader himself. The content is public and it costs nothing to serve, so there is no economic reason to know who is reading it.

The only honest way to protect a visitor is to make the visitor unnecessary. No login means no password to steal. No email means no list to leak. No profile means no profile to sell.

What I get in return

The statistics tell me that somebody from almost every country has opened this site, and I find that beautiful. Somebody in a city I will never visit read a paragraph I wrote at my desk. I do not know the name, the face or the reason, and I do not need them to be grateful.

That is the trade. You keep your identity, I keep the text, and nobody keeps anything else. ;)
