Water · the habitat that covers most of the planet

The Water Index

Life began in water and never left it: fishes, sharks and rays, whales that went back, octopuses, crabs, corals that build reefs, kelp that grows into forests, and single cells that hold up the whole food web. This page sorts them by group, order and family, and records for each one whether it belongs to salt water, fresh water, or both.

Every kingdom is here. Not only fishes: aquatic plants, seaweeds, plankton, protists, cyanobacteria, water fungi, sponges, worms and water insects share the catalogue, because a habitat is not a taxonomic rank.
