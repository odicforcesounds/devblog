The Plant Index

A plant cannot walk away from the thing that wants to eat it, so it argues instead: with spines, with tannin, with a field of poison in a leaf. Every card here answers the same question first — can this be eaten — and then shows the sentence the answer came from.

Read this first. The verdict is what the encyclopaedia records, not a recommendation. Edible plants have deadly lookalikes, parts differ from parts, and preparation rules exist for a reason. Never eat a plant because a page said so.
