Religions and spiritual schools

Every way the world was explained

Eleven shelves, from the burials that left no scripture to the religions invented last century. Each shelf holds its branches — a tradition, a denomination, a school of argument — and each branch holds the named paths inside it, with the first sentence the encyclopaedia writes about them and a photograph where one exists. The order is roughly chronological, and the grouping is this index's own: a tradition appears once, on the shelf where somebody looking for it would start.

Opening the catalogue…

Two letters are enough; the shelves are read only when you search.

Sentences and photographs: Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, fetched when this catalogue was compiled; every entry links back to its own article. The shelves, their order and their names are this index's own reading, offered as a way to find things and not as a claim about who owns whom.

Built in the same spirit as the world index, and drawn from the flow of things a person still asks about.
