I feel boring, so I am asking your help :)

2026-05-24 — —

This page is an invitation, and it is also an experiment. I have a small static site, no database, no login, no users, and I would like to see what happens when someone treats it as a target. Everything below is the honest version of why.

The main objective is that you attack my sites and I redirect your IP to somewhere. Since you can change Your IP, you can attack me from many others. So, at the end of this challenge, I will be more "active" and you too. But since is not always easy to convince someone to attack some site, I was thinking about insulting you. I mean, I think you are too Week in Intelligence Level to be able to hack my sites, or maybe you are too Stupid, which I believe you are. So, yeah, if you don't mind, try to hack my sites, while I will be monitoring any http request to understand if I should block you or block the Entire Country, while maybe I will not, since I like to piss you off. with my awesome content that is not mine at all, but yours, just by reading it and saving it in your Memory. As You can see.. I am always thinking about how to interact with new people in a peaceful way. I mean, internet attacks are peaceful too... like You are to my sites. ;)

For example, I think there is some guy from France that is always trying to attack my sites, but the guy is a Lover of PHP, which means that this user think that all sites in the world are made with PHP. That is the reason that let me think about how Stupid this user is. Some others from Netherlands are the same. USA is the same. I mean, they are all the same. Stupid! Unable to use the tools that they have in front of them, to read the Source Code of this sites and Guess what they need to do.

Another interesting activity is from people who think that all sites use some kind of JavaScript Framework, or Ruby or Python or with any other language where you can export Server variables using the .env files. This are less stupid than the first ones, even if they continue to be stupid to me. :)

The one the is less stupid is someone who think that all sites in the world are hosted on some dedicated server or VPS and try to find the /etc/ folder from a UNIX/BSD/Linux machines. This ones are the less stupid, but yeah, Stupid enougth to Fail! :)

But the most stupid ones are those who think I need a login system to create new posts or even worst, they think that I am using some kind of Database. Those are the Highest of Stupidity! The Kings of Stupidity. LoooL

The rules, such as they are

There is no form, no prize and no scoreboard. But if you accept the game, these are the terms I keep:

  • I do not fight back. I read the log, I close what is open, I continue writing.
  • I do not publish your address. The log is a lesson for me, not a list of names.
  • I do not call anyone. A threat of police on a static page is theatre, and bad theatre.
  • If you find a real problem, tell me and I will fix it. That is worth more than the attack.

What each kind of attempt teaches me

A scanner looking for wp-login.php teaches me that most of the web is built on one product and that its users are the majority of the targets. That has nothing to do with me, and everything to do with how a monoculture behaves: one crack, a million doors.

The one who looks for /etc/passwd teaches me that people still believe every URL is a window into a file system. That was true in 1998, in a very specific kind of server, and it is a museum piece now. It is also the attempt I respect the most, because it at least guesses that there is a system underneath.

The one who searches for .env teaches me about the industry. It means that somewhere, on some framework, a secret was stored in a text file that the server happily hands out. That is not an attacker's mistake. That is a default, and defaults are what most people ship.

What I actually learn, and what I do not

I learn which paths are worth watching, how fast a botnet moves, how a challenge page changes the shape of the traffic. I do not learn anything about my readers, because I do not collect them, and this is the part where the experiment becomes funny: the only people who reveal themselves on this site are the ones who arrive without permission.

There is also a less comfortable lesson. A site this small receives attacks every day without being interesting. Multiply that by every site in the world and the amount of wasted electricity becomes absurd. Most of the noise on the Internet is not crime, it is habit.

An invitation with a smile

So yes: if you are bored, try. Use the tools you have, read the source, and notice how little there is to take. The worst that can happen is that I learn something and you spend an afternoon. The best is that you discover how a static page is built, and build one yourself, without a database, without a framework, and without asking a platform for permission to exist.

And if you are one of the ones who arrives with a browser and reads to the end of the page: you are the traffic I was writing for. The other ones only make the logs interesting. ;)
