The Insect Index

Four out of five known animals are insects, and the rest of this page is the company they keep: spiders with wings that never existed, scorpions under stones, centipedes in leaf litter, woodlice rolling up in the light. Each card says how the animal grows up — in one shape, or through a larva and a pupa.

Not only insects. "Similar" here means the other arthropods and near-arthropods most people put in the same mental box: spiders, scorpions, ticks and mites, harvestmen, camel spiders, centipedes, millipedes, woodlice, springtails and velvet worms.
