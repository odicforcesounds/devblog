The Bird Index

Birds are the one class of animals that answers back: a song at dawn, a call over water, a drum on dead wood. This page sorts them three ways — by the family tree, by where they live, and by what they do — with a photograph and, where one has been recorded, a voice.

Names and measurements follow the same source, so a figure that is missing there is missing here rather than guessed.
