Birds · ten thousand species, one catalogue

The Bird Index

Birds are the one class of animals that answers back: a song at dawn, a call over water, a drum on dead wood. This page sorts them three ways — by the family tree, by where they live, and by what they do — with a photograph and, where one has been recorded, a voice.

Opening the catalogue…

Type two letters or more; the order files are read only when you search.

Photographs, introductions, songs and calls come from Wikipedia and its media library, fetched through the public API when this catalogue was compiled; every card links back to the article and the file it uses. Names and measurements follow the same source, so a figure that is missing there is missing here rather than guessed.
