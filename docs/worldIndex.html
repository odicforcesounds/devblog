World · countries, their regions, their places

World Index

Every country on Earth in one list, each opening into its regions and each region into its towns. A place is here because people live in it, not because a guide book liked it: five hundred inhabitants is the only door. The order inside a country is population, so the largest city is always the first line.

What a place means here. The name, the region and the population come from the GeoNames dump, which records every populated place its contributors have found; the page behind each name comes from Wikipedia. A city of ten million and a village of five hundred sit in the same shelf, told apart only by the word beside them and the number on the right. A missing village means the dump has not reached it, not that nobody lives there.

Find a place

Two letters are enough. The lookup reads one letter-sized shard of the same dump the shelves come from, so a village can be found without opening its country.

Names, regions and populations come from the GeoNames dump, published under CC BY 4.0; the shelves and their order are this catalogue's own. Each country's photograph and introduction, and the article each place links to, come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons; a place with no article of its own opens a Wikipedia search for it, so every name on this page leads there. Places below five hundred people are not in the dump this page reads.
