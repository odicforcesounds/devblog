World Index

Every country on Earth in one list, each opening into its regions and each region into its towns. A place is here because people live in it, not because a guide book liked it: five hundred inhabitants is the only door. The order inside a country is population, so the largest city is always the first line.

What a place means here. The name, the region, the population and the page behind it come from the GeoNames dump, which records every populated place its contributors have found. A city of ten million and a village of five hundred sit in the same shelf, told apart only by the word beside them and the number on the right. A missing village means the dump has not reached it, not that nobody lives there.
