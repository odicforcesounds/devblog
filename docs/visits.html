Domain visits II

I pass some weeks without watching the statistics of this domain.

I think the number of visits continue to grow, depending on the days, mostly in the middle of the week, not in the weeken.

Strange, but is what the numbers say.

Singapore continue to explore the videos (is anyone there who understand Portuguese?)

Hong Kong continue to explore all other domains.

Canada too, Portugal too. USA, Germany, Turkey, India, Brasil, etc..

I think people didn’t register into Github, to share some comments with me, so…

I continue to be the Monkey who doesn’t listen, while I can read but no one write me a bit.

Enjoy the Show! :D

What the pattern suggests

A real audience has a rhythm. People read during the week, at work or between two tasks, and they disappear on the weekend because the weekend belongs to something else. A machine does not care about Saturdays, although a lot of automated traffic slows down when the offices that host it are closed.

So a curve that falls on Saturday can mean readers, and it can also mean crawlers running on company infrastructure. I cannot separate the two with the summary alone, and that uncertainty is the honest state of anyone who looks at a dashboard of a small site.

The geography of a small blog

Singapore looking at the videos, Hong Kong looking at everything, Canada, Portugal, the United States, Germany, Turkey, India, Brazil. A list like that says more about the network than about the audience. Big hosting providers sit in a few countries, and their addresses make the map look global even when the visitor is one room away.

And yet the question about Portuguese remains. Somebody in Singapore is opening pages in a language that is not the language of the country, which is either a very patient reader or a very confused proxy.

The silence of the reader

Nobody writes. Not because nobody reads, but because writing costs something and reading does not. To leave a comment you need an account, and to create an account you need a reason, and a reason is exactly what a stranger does not have.

So I keep writing and I keep reading the silence, which is the most common form of feedback on the Internet. The Monkey who doesn't listen is also the Monkey who doesn't demand. Enjoy the Show. :D
