Since there is few elements that are too bright





You can save Your Eyes dear reader of this blog.
So, is what I did.
I change colors for this site and now it hurts less any eye.





What changed

The background went dark, the text changed from pure white to a softer tone, and the elements that used to scream for attention now sit quietly in a corner. The page did not lose content, it lost glare.

This is not only a matter of taste. A bright screen in a dark room forces the eye to adapt constantly, and the result is the tired feeling you get after twenty minutes of reading a page that has nothing wrong except its contrast.

The rules I kept

Text must stay readable in daylight. Dark does not mean invisible, so the body text keeps enough contrast to be read comfortably, and the accents stay saturated so a link is still a link. Too little contrast is the opposite mistake, and a fashionable one: a page that looks elegant and cannot be read is not a dark theme, it is a low resolution problem.

For the reader

If your eyes still hurt, the screen brightness is doing more damage than the colors. Lower the brightness at night, keep the page at a reasonable distance, and read shorter pages more often. The blog is only doing its part.

You can save your eyes, dear reader. So, is what I did.
