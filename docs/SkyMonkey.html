🐵 the monkey and all his friends
🐵 hi. i am monkey. i like to climb and drop things and ask why. this is a story about me and all my friends and how we all changed. it is very long. you can read a little and then more. just go slow like a snail sometimes.
🐵 one day i woke up and the sky was too high. i tried to climb to it and i fell on soft grass. grass said ouch but in a nice way. i said sorry grass. then i thought, what if up is not the only way. what if side to side is also good. i took my ladder and i put it flat on the ground. now it is a bridge. come on, walk on me, i said to the ants. and they did.
🐭 rat came first. rat is small and fast and smart. people say rat is first because rat was smart and rode on big ox. rat has a bag full of old stuff. buttons and corn and a toy car with one wheel. rat said i am first so i get more. i said first to what. rat stopped. no one asked that before. i gave rat half my banana even if rat was first. rat gave me half back. we both had whole happy in our tummies. rat said maybe first can mean first to give. we laughed and our teeth showed.
🐮 ox came next with big heavy bags. ox is so strong. ox can pull a whole house. but ox looked tired. i climbed on the bags and said what is in here. ox said rocks and rain and old words like you must be heavy to be good. i said let us open. we opened and the wind took the old words away. ox stood up light. ox danced. boom boom. the ground shook but happy. ox said i can still be strong and still rest. i said yes. strong is not just heavy. strong is also soft.
🐯 tiger came loud. ROAR. i covered my ears. leaves fell. tiger roared hello and roared bye and roared i am hungry. i said can you say hi very small. tiger tried and only air came out. we tried again and again. at last tiger said hi like a baby cat. we both giggled. tiger said when i am loud all the time no one hears my small sad. i said you can be loud and soft. you have stripes and soft paws. both are you.
🐰 rabbit was hiding behind the moon grass. rabbit is fast but shy. rabbit said do not look at me. i said i will look at the cloud next to you. rabbit came out a little. we played stay. we stayed side by side for ten breaths. one two three. rabbit wanted to run at seven. i breathed loud like a train to make rabbit laugh. rabbit stayed to ten. rabbit left little white stones so we can find rabbit next time. rabbit said i am still scared but now i can be found.
🐲 dragon came from the sky with shiny skin and fire that smelled like birthday candles. dragon said i fly high because i can. i said can i come. dragon said no one sits with dragon. i sat near the tail where it is not hot. up high the world looked small like toys. dragon only flew in a circle to show off. i said do you want to smell rice down there. dragon said low is not for me. we went low anyway. wow. rice waved and people waved. dragon felt warm in the heart, not hot fire warm, soft warm. dragon landed bump crash. sorry, said dragon with smoke that made the word sorry. we all said it is ok. now dragon flies high sometimes and low a lot.
🐍 snake knows a lot. snake knows where the river sleeps and why grandma sings. snake said do not hug me i am cool and wise. i hugged fast before snake could make a knot. snake went stiff then soft one loop at a time. snake said you are warm like sun on rock. i said you can be wise and warm. after that snake told stories to help sleep, not to show off. pig could not sleep and snake said your bad dream about teeth means you are growing. pig slept. snake still says sssss when scared but now snake says that was scared sss not mean sss.
🐴 horse runs fast fast fast. wind in face. but horse had a sad little rock inside that bounced when running. i stood in the field with my arms wide and said stop i made slow cake. horse stopped with dust everywhere. what is slow cake, said horse. this, i said. we sat. we watched a snail. snail won a race with no one. horse cried sitting still. tears fell faster than running. i said sad rides with you when you run. give sad a carrot. horse named sad pebble. now horse runs for fun and also naps. naps are runs inside.
🐐 goat eats all. paper, cans, my homework once. goat also eats worries. tell me, says goat, munch munch. soon goat tummy hurts full of other people fears. i brought baskets. spit out, i said, not to throw, to look. we put worries on grass like socks. this one says too slow. this one says too loud. this one is not even yours, it is mine from last year. sun made them small. goat learned to take small bites. goat said i can sit with you while you chew your own worry. goat milk now makes sweet cheese with holes. holes let air in. holes are good.
🐓 rooster wakes the sun. cock a doodle doo every morning. throat hurts. eyes sleepy. rooster thinks if i do not scream sun will not come. i stayed up all night with rooster eating warm corn. morning came pink with no scream. rooster mouth open. it came anyway, said rooster. i said sun likes your song but does not need it to live. rooster cried happy and then screamed happy just for fun. now rooster sometimes sings, sometimes quiet, sometimes naps at noon with comb flat. sun still comes. you are loved, not used.
🐶 dog guards all. house, bone, old mad from long ago. woof woof at mail and moon. at night dog runs after own tail worry worry. i said let us bury that old bone together. we dug a hole under the plum tree. we said thanks for the lesson bye for the hurt. dog sniffed flowers after. i said who guards you when you guard all. dog said can i guard my own sleep. yes. dog now barks less wags more. dog lets friends guard dog too. tail still there but now for happy.
🐷 pig loves much. mud, soup, naps, rain on ears. people said too much, be less. pig tried to love less and got thin and gray. i jumped in mud splash big. love more but share more, i said with mud face. pig made a huge pot of soup for all. rat cheese in, rabbit carrot in, snake hot spice in, tiger quiet in. soup tasted like us. pig learned to sleep well to love well, to say no sometimes to say yes true. pig naps big now with ear flips. joy is not extra. joy is rice. you need it each day.
🌙 then the wind came whooooo and knocked all ladders down. bang crash clack. we all ran out in sleep clothes. dragon sleep clothes huge, rat sleep clothes tiny with cheese dots. the gold sign that said climb climb climb broke in two. who did this, screamed rooster. me, said wind. i was bored of up. we were quiet. then i laughed. i put my ladder flat again. one by one all put ladders flat. flat flat flat. now we had a big web of wood to sit on. we sat in a ring. we ate cheese and soup. we told small stories. tiger said hi small. rabbit breathed ten breaths. horse sat still. snake told warm tale. goat shared cheese with holes. rooster sang low. dog rested head on ox foot. dragon flew low just over our heads to make cool wind. pig snored happy. sky saw and put one new star on. just one. enough.
🐵 after that thunder came mad. boom i change nothing without paper, said thunder. i gave thunder a soft blanket. thunder sat, confused. blanket made echo soft. i said i did not change them. they changed like milk when you stop shaking. thunder thought boom boom soft boom. that night thunder rained soft not loud. plants said thanks. roofs said thanks.
🎁 next day on the bridge wood there were gifts with no names. a shiny dragon skin piece for brave to land. a whole snake skin with no knots for growing. an old horse shoe worn on one side for stopping to rest. i put them in my pocket with a hole. my pocket sings. rattle clink. rat put a button in. pig put soup smell in. dog put a flower from the bone tree in. if you find a gift on your road, leave one too. bridges need kind tolls.
💤 that night we dreamed new dreams. rooster dreamed sun said thanks for songs no rush. dog dreamed bone flower grew to a tree with doors for all, even mail man. pig dreamed soup sea with bread boats, no one sank because soup holds. rabbit dreamed she stayed to eleven breaths. ox dreamed light bags with feathers. tiger dreamed purr that fixed bones. rat dreamed big pantry with doors open. snake dreamed hug that did not squeeze too much. horse dreamed nap that ran far inside. goat dreamed empty tummy happy tummy. dragon dreamed rice waving. i dreamed nothing. black soft empty. my favorite. empty leaves room for your dream to come visit.
🌞 morning came flat like light bridge across the floor. twelve small bowls hot steam up up. twelve shoes by the door all mixed. one tiny shoe with lights that blink when you run. one big boot dirty from dancing. one stripe sock alone. one moon slipper. one rain boot for jumping sky water. one quiet slipper with no sound. one run shoe with lace open. one chewed sandal. one too big shoe to grow in, that is mine. one clean shoe with straight laces. one shoe that waits even when no one home. one soft slipper that smells like soup. twelve cups with milk rings. orange peel in a long twist. blanket house where ladders were. gold sign now painted by small hands says climb if you want or bridge or nap. wood does not mind. sky does not mind. we do not mind.
✏️ on the table a note in bendy letters says we changed how we think real is. real is not just up. real is also side to side and with and soft. come back tonight. bring pocket band. i read it out loud slow. wind took it to the plum tree. sun came pink on time, pretending rooster called it, to be kind.
🐵 and we are still here. i still drop things. rat still saves. ox still dances bad good. tiger still says hi small each day to practice. rabbit leaves stones so we can find her. dragon lands more than flies. snake hugs back now. horse sits when sad sits too. goat chews slow. rooster naps at noon. dog sleeps safe. pig shares soup. we fight sometimes. tiger roars too loud and rabbit hides. dragon knocks things with tail. horse runs when we say sit. i take bridges and make ladders by mistake. then we fix. fix is also play.
🍊 if you want to play with us, take an orange and try to cut in twelve same pieces. you cannot. hands small. knife big. pieces bendy. good. now give pieces out. bigger to small, smaller to big, biggest to you. taste. see. same sweet. that is the whole secret. not same size. same sweet. grown ups forget. we remind.
🌧️ sometimes rain comes and bridge wood gets wet and slippery. we do not run. we sit under big leaf dragon holds. snake tells where river sleeps. horse breaths slow so we all breath slow. goat hums. dog leans. pig shares warm corn. rat tells map where north smells like cheese. tiger purrs low. rabbit counts breaths for us one to ten. rooster sings rain song not sun song. monkey, that is me, peels orange. rain stops when we are not looking. that is how rain likes to leave, quiet.
⭐ at night sky leans close. you can hear stars blink if you stop talking. we stop talking. shhh. hear. blink blink. one star blinks for rat, one for ox, one for tiger, one for rabbit, one for dragon, one for snake, one for horse, one for goat, one for me monkey, one for rooster, one for dog, one for pig. twelve blinks. then one more blink for you listening. yes you. sky saved one for you. did you hear. shhh again. there it is.
🪞 one day we found a big mirror in the grass. tiger roared at it. rabbit hid from it. dragon tried to fly into it. i touched it. cold. rat said who is that small smart one. ox said who is that strong tired one. we all saw us but small. not big zodiac in sky, small feet on ground. shoes untied. milk on lip. snake said mirror is true when it makes you kind. we left mirror there for wind to look. wind looked and said oh i am just air moving. and moved happy.
🕰️ rat has a clock that goes back two times a day right. we played back day. we woke up at night and ate breakfast for dinner and said good morning to the moon. pig loved it. rooster was confused and sang at wrong time and we all laughed and sun laughed pink. ox napped at breakfast. horse ran slow motion. it taught us time is not a boss. time is soup. you can stir.
🌊 we went to see river where snake says river sleeps. river was wide and sang shhh. we put leaf boats in. each leaf had a worry drawn. goat worry too much eat. tiger worry too loud. rabbit worry hide. dragon worry high. my worry drop things. we watched leaves float away. not gone, just moving. dog wanted to swim to save leaves. we said let them go. saving is sometimes letting float. dog sat on hands to practice. good dog.
🍲 pig soup day is best day. pig wakes early and chops and sings chop chop love love. each brings one thing. rat brings corn. ox brings big potato. tiger brings quiet, hard to wrap but pig finds a way. rabbit brings carrot tops. dragon brings warm breath to start fire, careful small. snake brings spice one drop not more. horse brings fast water from well. goat brings herbs sorted. rooster brings morning egg. dog brings hunger, best spice. monkey brings oranges for after. we stir big pot with oar from bridge wood. soup says blub blub. we eat and our spoons clink like pocket band. full tummies, full hearts.
🪁 dragon made kites from old ladder signs. climb climb words now fly. we ran with horse fast to lift them. tiger held string soft not roar pull. rabbit watched from blanket fort safe and happy. snake tied knots that do not slip. goat chewed extra string into soft. rooster crowed wind come. dog chased shadows of kites. pig napped under kites shade. rat rode kite tail for one second brave. i swung from kite string and fell in hay. hay said oof nice. sky said more color please. we gave more.
🌱 we planted plum stones from dog tree. ox dug with nose strong puff. rabbit patted soft. pig watered with soup ladle by mistake, oops, still grew. snake told seeds sleep now wake later. horse stood still to shade them, hard for horse, good practice. goat did not eat them, big love. rooster sang grow grow. dog guarded without bark. dragon breathed warm not hot. rat mapped them with button markers. i talked to them each day hi small grow slow. one sprout came up bent. we said bent is also up. bent sprout grew brave.
💡 night came and lights went out poof. dark dark. rabbit scared. tiger wanted to roar light on. i said shhh let eyes grow. we sat. eyes grew like cats. we saw star blink blink twelve plus you. we heard ox breath big calm. we felt dog warm side. snake told dark stories soft. horse did not run. goat hummed low. pig breathed soup dreams. rooster whispered cock a doodle soon but not yet. dragon tail glowed a little, enough. dark is not bad. dark is blanket for sky. limits are like dark. they hold you so you can rest.
🧦 tiger lost stripe sock again. we searched. rat checked pantry. ox lifted bags. rabbit checked moon pocket. dragon checked clouds. snake checked knots. horse ran far to look. goat ate a clue by mistake sorry. rooster crowed have you seen sock. dog sniffed and found it on pig soup pot as cozy. we all laughed. tiger wore mismatched socks proud. different is good. i wear too big shoes to grow in. you grow into different each day.
📦 we made a box for mad. when mad comes, put mad in box, not in friend. tiger puts roar in box. roar shakes box. funny. horse puts run in box, box jogs. dragon puts fire in box, box warm. goat puts munch in box. rat puts rush in box. i put throw in box. then we sit with box till box calm. then we open and talk. box taught us feelings visit, they do not live. you are house, not storm.
💌 we write notes in bendy letters. rat writes thanks for bigger half. ox writes i napped good. tiger writes hi small sorry loud. rabbit writes here-ish with stone dot. dragon writes low flight best. snake writes warm hug ok. horse writes pebble ate carrot. goat writes slow chew sweet. rooster writes sun came anyway love. dog writes guarded sleep good. pig writes soup holds all. monkey writes bridge not ladder today. we stick notes on plum tree. tree wears words like leaves. wind reads and cries happy rain one drop.
🐵 so if you feel small, good. small hands share best. if you feel big, good. big can land soft. if you feel loud, practice hi small. if you feel shy, leave stones. if you feel fast sad, name it pebble. if you feel full of worries, sort like socks. if you feel must scream sun up, try quiet once. if you guard all, guard your sleep too. if you love much, share much. that is all we learned. that is a lot. sky thinks new now. not just up up up. also side to side and with and soft. come sit on our bridge wood. bring orange. we saved sweet piece for you. you are the thirteenth blink. shhh. hear. there.
