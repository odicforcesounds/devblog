The soul cannot escape from the body and the body can live without the soul

The self who sings about the self, is being true to the soul and to the universe full of many other souls. Souls that can find them self's in another self, since that is the condition of all living beings in this musical world, where electromagnetism (from the body it self) take us, somewhere else, or else, we would even create evolution, we wouldn't even break the rules, to understand the limits and create a better condition for the self and to many other selfs.

A cell with a window

Every self lives inside itself and cannot leave. The body can be touched, measured and moved, but the one who looks through the eyes has never been seen by anyone else. We assume that another person is inhabited, because we speak to them and they answer, but the answer comes through the same door we use, so the assumption stays an assumption.

This is the jail. Not a punishment, a condition. The wall is the difference between what I experience and what I can express.

The body does not need the soul to keep going

A heart beats while the mind is asleep, a wound closes while the person is not paying attention, a body can live for years when the one who used to inhabit it is no longer there. That is the uncomfortable half of the sentence: the machine runs without the operator.

The other half is the one that gives me hope. The soul cannot leave the body, which means every meeting between two people is a meeting of two jails, and every word that manages to cross the wall is an achievement and not a formality.

Why music crosses more easily

Words need to be translated, but a melody does not. Two people who do not share a language can listen to the same track and agree about the feeling, which is the closest thing to a direct connection that we have. The body produces electromagnetism, the strings produce sound, and for a few minutes the cell has a window.

Maybe that is the whole story of this blog. I sing about the self, and you read about your own. The words are mine, the recognition is yours, and in that exchange two jails become one room.
