Section I / The Indictment

The Monstrous Lie of "Honorable Warfare" and the Cowardice of Earth-Bound Militaries

Let us strip away the medals, the marching bands, the polished brass, and the slick promotional videos produced by your defense ministries. Let us look with raw, unblinking human eyes at what you actually do. You—the generals, the admirals, the strategists, and the soldiers who obey them—claim to belong to an honorable profession. You speak of duty, courage, and defense.

"You are not fighting honorable duels. You are detonating high explosives in the middle of living rooms, nurseries, and farm fields, pretending that 'collateral damage' is just a regretful footnote in your tactical playbooks."

It is a disgusting, cowardly lie. In ancient times, warriors met in open fields to clash steel against steel. Today, you sit in air-conditioned bunkers or armored command posts, launching million-dollar hypersonic missiles and drone strikes into densely populated cities. You test weapons of horrific precision and catastrophic reach on the very cradle that nurtures human life—our only home in an unforgiving universe.

You claim to be defending citizens, yet your methods destroy the water they drink, the air they breathe, and the topsoil that feeds them. You spend trillions of dollars perfecting tools whose sole logical outcome is the mutilation of flesh and the annihilation of infrastructure. It is sick, it is grotesque, and it is a complete betrayal of the human capacity to feel, to empathy, and to build.

Section II / Moral Outrage

Children Don't Wish to Die for Your Business and Control

Only humans possess the gift of profound feeling, of conscious love, of grief, and of creative wonder. And yet, you treat human beings as raw material—as statistics, as target packages, as acceptable casualties on a geopolitical spreadsheet.

What business does a four-year-old child have with your oil pipelines, your mineral rights, your maritime borders, or your ideological disputes? None. A child does not care about your national flags or your corporate defense contracts. They wish to play, to laugh, to learn, and to grow under a clean sky. Yet when your artillery barrages rain down, when your white phosphorus burns through roofs, when your landmines sit buried in fields waiting for little feet—it is the innocent who pay the ultimate price.

The Fundamental Asymmetry of War on Earth

If two soldiers wish to fight each other, that is their tragedy. But modern warfare is never restricted to soldiers. It slaughters the elderly who cannot flee, mothers clutching infants, doctors in hospitals, and species of wildlife already teetering on the brink of extinction. To wage war on Earth is to hold the entire human family and biosphere hostage to your childish, aggressive ego.

Governments strictly forbid their citizens from committing violence, threatening neighbours, or destroying property. Yet those same governments mobilize entire industrial complexes to commit mass murder across borders. This is not governance; it is state-sanctioned madness fueled by a desperate hunger for possession and control.
